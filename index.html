Quick answers

Sync between devices? Sign in to iCloud on both and turn on iCloud for Noting.
No reminders? Check Settings › Notifications › Noting.
Delete my data? Delete the app; remove the iCloud copy in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage › Noting.
